# Sibambo Editor add-on

Start with `describe_scene` to inspect the open document, revision, mode and the owner's selection.
Read `quickstart` for the connection and editing workflow and `errors` for recovery.
Only the owner's Editor holds geometry; the service relays calls and records sessions.

This WP2 delivery supplies the transport. Architecture, freeform modelling, perception and floor-plan guide topics are completed in WP10 after their Editor implementations. A connected transport alone does not prove those capabilities.
